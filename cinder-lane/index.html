01 — Our story

One fire. No shortcuts.

Cinder Lane is an imagined neighbourhood restaurant on a quiet corner. The idea is simple: everything on the menu touches the hearth — the bread, the vegetables, even the ice cream's caramel.

The menu changes with what the market brings each week, so the kitchen keeps it short and lets the fire do the talking.

  • 900°C at the heart of the fire
  • 12hours of slow ember roasting for the lamb
  • 8dishes on the weekly menu, never more

03 — The hearth

From log to plate.

  1. I

    Light at noon

    The oak is lit five hours before service so the coals settle into an even, glowing bed.

  2. II

    Read the heat

    Flames for flatbreads, coals for fish, the dying embers for slow roots and caramel.

  3. III

    Rest & share

    Everything comes to the table to be passed around — the way food over a fire should be.

Good to know

Do you take walk-ins?

Yes — the counter is kept for walk-ins every night.

Are there vegetarian options?

Around half of the menu is vegetarian, and the kitchen can adapt most plates.

Can I book the whole room?

Private dining is available on Mondays and Tuesdays for groups of 20–42.

Is Cinder Lane a real restaurant?

No. Cinder Lane Kitchen is a fictional business created to show a one-page website design.
