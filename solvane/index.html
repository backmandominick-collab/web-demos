/ faq

Questions, answered.

Does my roof need to face south?

No. East and west roofs work well too — they simply spread generation across the day.

What happens on cloudy days?

Panels still generate in daylight, just less. The battery and grid cover the difference.

Can I add a battery later?

Yes. Systems are designed so storage can be added without replacing the inverter.

Is Solvane a real company?

No. Solvane Energy is a fictional business created to demonstrate a one-page website design.
