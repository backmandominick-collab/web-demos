FAQ

I've never done pilates. Where do I start?

Mat Foundations is designed for complete beginners — arrive ten minutes early and say hello.

What should I bring?

Comfortable clothes and grip socks. Mats, water and towels are provided.

How do cancellations work?

Cancel up to 12 hours before class and your credit returns automatically.

No. Tidewell is a fictional business created to demonstrate a one-page website design.
